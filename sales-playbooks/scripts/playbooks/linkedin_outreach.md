# LinkedIn Outreach (through Linkedroid)

LinkedIn actions go through the owner's connected Linkedroid tools (`linkedin_search_profiles`,
`linkedin_get_profile`, `linkedin_send_connection`, `linkedin_send_message`, `linkedin_list_conversations`,
`linkedin_read_conversation`, `linkedin_like_post`, `linkedin_comment_on_post`). Never browser automation,
never a second profile, never private data beyond name, title, company and public posts.

## Risk and ethics first

- The account is the owner's real identity; act only as the configured persona.
- Asked whether messages are automated: answer truthfully, at once.
- "Not interested" or an ignored thread is final, and the opt-out follows the person across channels.
- Every message is shown to the owner and approved before it goes, like email.

## Connection requests

No note accepts slightly more often than a generic note. Use a note only with something genuinely
relevant, under 300 characters:
"Hi {{first_name}}, saw your post about {{topic}}. Would like to connect." · "{{first_name}}, we're both
in {{industry}}. Always good to connect with fellow {{title}}s."
Never a pitch on connect ("I'd love to tell you about our product").

## After they accept

- Day 0: don't pitch. Optionally react to a recent post.
- Day 1-2: thank them, ask one genuine question or share one relevant insight. No pitch, no link.
- Day 4-5, if they replied: continue naturally; introduce the product only through a natural opening, framed as "we built something for exactly that".
- Day 4-5, if silent: one follow-up that adds value (a relevant article or observation). Then stop; move to email if you have an address.

## Warm-up for high-value prospects

Week 1: view profile. Week 1-2: react to two or three posts. Week 2: one thoughtful comment (substance,
one follow-up question, under three sentences; never "Great post!"). Week 2-3: connection request.

## Limits (Linkedroid enforces its own; stay under these)

Connection requests 20/day, 100/week · new-connection messages 10/day · profile views 50/day · spread
through the owner's working hours · respect `quiet_hours`. Check `linkedin_account_health` before a
batch; stop the day if it reports warnings.

## Personalisation sources

Recent posts, a job change (new role = receptive window), company milestones, mutual connections,
shared groups, their About section, featured content.
